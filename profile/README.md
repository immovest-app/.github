**Immovest** is a minimalist web tool designed for people who want to invest in real estate with a clear head. No spreadsheets, no jargon. Just a simple way to see how much you can borrow, assess a property and calculate its return.

Built and maintained by [Victor Mangeleer](https://vikvador.github.io).
